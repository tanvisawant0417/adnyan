OUR JOURNEY

From Humble
Beginnings

Adnyan was founded by a group of passionate individuals in Mumbai with a vision to make a meaningful difference in society.

Founded in Mumbai, the organization began by addressing early challenges through community engagement, education and support.

With dedicated efforts and the support of volunteers and well-wishers, Adnyan gradually expanded its work beyond its initial efforts to reach multiple districts across Maharashtra.

The Trust continues to work towards education, creativity, community development and meaningful social change.
